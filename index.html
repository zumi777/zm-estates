The firm

We'd rather place one right home than list a hundred we don't believe in.

ZM Real Estate is a boutique Dubai brokerage. We keep our book small on purpose, so that every client works with a principal who actually knows the building, the developer's track record, and what the unit next door really traded for.

We were founded by operators who bought and sold their own property in Dubai long before they advised on anyone else's. That's the lens we bring to every viewing — is this a good buy, honestly, or is it just a good story?

Boutique by choice, we're accountable for every number we put in front of you — and we'll tell you plainly when a deal isn't worth doing.
